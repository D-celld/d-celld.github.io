Local AI Image Studio for Mac

🎨 imagegen-mac - Private AI Image Generation on Your Mac

Imagine typing a few words and watching a picture appear — right on your own Mac, with no internet connection and no one else ever seeing your prompts. That is exactly what imagegen-mac does.

✨ What Is imagegen-mac?

imagegen-mac is a friendly desktop app for Mac computers that lets you create pictures from plain text descriptions. You type something like "a lighthouse on a stormy cliff at sunset" and the app draws it for you. You can also take a picture you already have and ask the app to change it — swap the background, adjust the colours, or turn a rough sketch into a polished illustration.

What makes it different from the image generators you find on the web is where the work happens. Everything runs on your own computer using a model called Qwen-Image 2.1. Nothing is uploaded. No account is needed. No credits are spent. You can be on a plane with the Wi-Fi switched off and it still works.

The app is built specifically for Apple Silicon Macs and uses the graphics power of those machines, so images come out quickly without making your fans roar. It also includes a built-in MCP server, which is a way for AI assistants such as Claude Code and other agent tools to call the image generator directly — but you never have to touch that part unless you want to.

🖼️ What You Can Create

Everything below happens inside the app, on your own machine:

  • Text to image. Describe a scene in ordinary words and get a finished picture in return. Longer, more specific descriptions give more control over the result.
  • Image editing. Bring in a photo or drawing and ask for changes. Clean up a background, extend the edges of a picture, or restyle an image entirely.
  • Style exploration. Try the same description with a few different looks — watercolour, photograph, pencil sketch, poster art — until you find the one you like.
  • Batch ideas. Produce several variations of one prompt so you can pick the strongest result instead of re-typing everything.
  • Agent access. The included MCP server lets Claude Code and similar AI assistants request images from imagegen-mac automatically, which is handy if you already work with those tools.

🔒 Your Images Stay Yours

When you use a website for image generation, your words and your pictures travel across the internet and land on someone else's computer. With imagegen-mac that journey never happens. Your prompts, your source photos, and your finished artwork stay in a folder on your own Mac.

That matters if you are working on client material, personal photos, or anything confidential — and it also simply means the app keeps working when your connection does not. No subscriptions, no daily limits, no watermarks, and no waiting in a queue behind thousands of other people.

🤖 Built-In MCP Server

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. In plain terms, it is a standard plug that lets AI assistants talk to other software. imagegen-mac ships with that plug already installed, so an assistant like Claude Code can ask the app for a picture and receive it back without you doing anything manually.

If you are not using AI agents yet, you can safely ignore this feature entirely — it sits quietly in the background and does not get in the way of normal use.

🖥️ What You Need

🧭 Your First Image in Five Steps

If you have never used an image generator before, here is a gentle way to begin.

1. Start simple. Type "a red fox sitting in fresh snow" and generate. You are learning what the app feels like, not making a masterpiece.

2. Add detail. Now try "a red fox sitting in fresh snow, soft morning light, shallow depth of field, photograph". Describe the light, the mood, and the style.

3. Change one thing at a time. Keep the wording the same and only swap the style word. Compare the results side by side.

4. Try an edit. Take a picture you already have and ask the app to change its background or colour palette.

5. Save your favourites. Keep the prompts that worked in a notes file. Good prompts are worth reusing, and small tweaks often produce your best images.

💡 Tips for Better Results

  • Describe what you want to see, not what you want to avoid.
  • Mention the light: golden hour, overcast, studio lighting, neon at night.
  • Mention the medium: photo, oil painting, ink drawing, 3D render.
  • Longer prompts are not always better. Three clear details usually beat fifteen vague ones.
  • Generate several variations before judging a prompt. Chance plays a part.

❓ Troubleshooting

The app is slow or the fans are loud. Close other heavy applications while generating. Large images use more memory than small ones.

macOS will not open the app. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and allow the app to run. This is a normal first-launch check.

The first launch takes a long time. The app is loading the image model onto your drive. Later launches start much faster.

Images look nothing like the description. Add concrete details about the subject, the setting, the light, and the style, then try again.

Disk space warning. Free up roughly 10 GB and restart the app.
